01 / THE EVENT

This is your
build weekend.

A student hackathon is a friendly, fast-paced event where teams turn an idea into a working project.

In 48 hours, you’ll team up with fellow students, learn by doing, and show your work to a panel of judges. No perfect idea or experience required—just curiosity and a willingness to build.
